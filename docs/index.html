Available as FreeLingo Cloud, with no installation, or self-hosted on your server.

Features

Everything in one structured learning loop.

01

Placement and study plans

Start with a CEFR assessment, then get a weekly roadmap adapted to your level, target language, and pace.

02

AI tutor chat and memory

Lingu remembers useful details across all your learning languages, while you can add, review, or delete memories from Settings.

03

Voice conversations

Put your lessons into conversation with Lingu. Start voice practice from a completed lesson, ask questions, and revisit the transcript afterwards.

04

Listening and reading

Generate level-aware audio and reading comprehension exercises that match your current study language.

05

Review and learning games

Review vocabulary with spaced-repetition flashcards, consult grammar and useful phrases, and reinforce your learning with Error Detective, Sentence Order, and Vocabulary Pairs.

06

Multi-language progress

Study Dutch, English, French, German, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Mainland Chinese, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, and Swedish side by side, with separate plans and progress for each language.

Cloud or self-hosted

Two ways to use FreeLingo.

Choose a managed service to focus on learning, or run your own installation for control over configuration and infrastructure.

Each installation has its own accounts and progress. Cloud links open the official service at freelingo.app; self-hosting links explain how to set up your own installation.

FreeLingo Cloud

We manage it. You learn.

Learn in your browser without installing or maintaining anything.

  • Hosting, maintenance and updates included.
  • AI and voice included within your plan's usage limits.
  • Start free without a card, with Premium available for more practice.
Try FreeLingo Cloud
Self-hosted

Your infrastructure. Your configuration.

Install FreeLingo on your server and choose your AI and voice providers.

  • Free, open-source software under AGPL-3.0. No FreeLingo Cloud subscription required; your administrator configures access and limits.
  • You handle setup, updates and backups, and cover infrastructure and provider costs.
  • Requires Docker Compose and configured AI and voice services; hardware and voice-language support depend on your providers.

External AI or voice providers receive the content they process. Self-hosting does not automatically make all processing local.
